# Next Step Conversations - Podcast

## Name
Next Step Conversations

## Description
Real conversations about the moves that matter most. Whether you're buying your first home, selling the one you've lived in for 30 years, or helping a parent figure out what comes next, Becca Pitts sits down with the people who make every step easier: lenders, inspectors, contractors, financial planners, elder law attorneys, in-home care providers, aging-in-place specialists, and more. Part real estate education, part senior transition guide, all honest talk about your next step.

## Host
Becca Pitts

## Scope
Covers both traditional real estate topics (buying, selling, investing, first-time buyers, market updates) AND senior transition education (aging in place, downsizing, care transitions, elder law, home modifications). The podcast serves as content for both yournextstepteam.com and yourbestseason.com.

## Guest Categories
- Lenders and mortgage brokers
- Home inspectors
- Contractors and home modification specialists
- Financial planners
- Elder law attorneys
- In-home care providers
- Aging-in-place specialists
- Senior living advisors
- Appraisers
- Real estate brokers and industry peers
- Families sharing their transition stories
