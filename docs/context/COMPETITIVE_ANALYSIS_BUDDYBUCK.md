# Competitive Analysis: BuddyBuck.com vs Your Next Step Home

## BuddyBuck.com Summary
- Buddy Buck, eXp Realty, Duvall WA (hyperlocal)
- 17 years, $42.16M sold, 100.56% sold-to-list, 43 five-star Google reviews
- Next.js site on Vercel (same stack we're using)
- Key features: stats bar, client story pages (/stories/[name]), Real Estate IQ Quiz, YouTube embedded on homepage, community media brand (Duvall TV, podcast, newsletter)
- Strong schema markup, SEO/GEO/AEO optimization

## What to Mirror (Structure)
- Stats bar with real numbers (years, transactions, reviews, SRES certification)
- Client stories as full dedicated pages (not just quote testimonials)
- Quiz/assessment as primary CTA (we have this)
- YouTube embedded on homepage
- "Any of these sound familiar?" scenario cards speaking to each ICP
- Separate buyer/seller intake paths
- Community media brand integration (Next Step Conversations podcast = our Duvall TV)
- Schema saturation (LocalBusiness, RealEstateAgent, Person, Review, BreadcrumbList)

## What NOT to Mirror (Visual/Brand)
- Not his yellow/black palette (we use BBCH palette: ink/moss/bone/sunshine)
- Not his illustrated logo style (we use DM Serif Display wordmark)
- Not his specific personal flourishes (onesies counter, husky references)
- Not his tone (his is louder/personality-forward; Becca's is warm/grounded/fun-quest)

## Becca's Strategic Advantages Over BuddyBuck
1. Three-business moat (YNSH + YBS + BBCH). Nobody else cross-links education, real estate, and care.
2. Senior transitions niche. Buddy serves general market. Becca serves the underserved senior segment.
3. SRES certification. Buddy doesn't have it.
4. Content depth: 15 blog articles on senior care fears. Buddy has 3 blog posts.
5. Podcast with professional guests (elder law, hospice, senior move management). Buddy's is community-focused.

## Key Positioning Update (June 2026)
Becca's primary niche is SENIOR TRANSITIONS, not general real estate. Blog and content should lead with senior-focused topics. Traditional buyer/seller content is secondary. Her reviews come from traditional work but her positioning goes forward as the senior transitions specialist.
