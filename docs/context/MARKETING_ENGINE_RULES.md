# Becca Pitts Marketing Engine - Project Context

## Strategic First Principles (non-negotiable)
- Build the converter first, then the traffic. The funnel must convert before we scale awareness.
- Own the audience. Email list and CRM are primary. Social platforms are distribution, not home base.
- 80/20 content split: 80% relatable/personal/community, 20% professional/CTA. People follow people, not brands.
- Data over assumptions. Every strategic decision is validated by performance data.
- 11 impressions in 90 days. Every person in Becca's sphere sees her at least 11 times across channels.

## Three-Website Architecture (all domains purchased)

**Site 1: Your Next Step Team** (yournextstepteam.com)
- Primary real estate hub for all ICPs
- Redirect: yournextsteprealty.com → yournextstepteam.com
- Content: Market updates, buyer/seller guides, neighborhood guides, assessment, vendor directory
- ManyChat keywords: MARKET, KEYS, CHECKLIST, NEIGHBORHOOD, INVEST, DREAM

**Site 2: Your Best Season** (yourbestseason.com)
- Senior transitions education hub (uncontested keyword space)
- Also purchased: downsizewashington.com (redirect TBD)
- Content: Downsizing guides, senior transition resources, interviews with senior services leaders
- ManyChat keywords: DOWNSIZE, VALUE
- Cross-links to yournextstepteam.com and burienbestcarehome.com

**Site 3: Burien Best Care Home** (burienbestcarehome.com)
- Adult family home business site
- Redirect: burienadultfamilyhome.com → burienbestcarehome.com
- Content: Care services, facility info, family resources, testimonials
- Pipeline: AFH families become real estate leads when they sell the family home

## ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles)
- **First-time buyers:** Millennials/Gen Z, 25-38, anxious, Instagram/TikTok/YouTube
- **Seniors/downsizers + adult children:** Boomers 60+ and Gen X 38-55, Facebook/YouTube/email. THIS IS BECCA'S PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATING ICP.
- **Move-up buyers:** Gen X/elder millennials, 35-55, LinkedIn/YouTube/Instagram
- **Sellers:** All ages, Facebook/LinkedIn/email/YouTube
- **Senior care residents:** Families of AFH residents, King County focus

## Tech Stack
BoldTrail (CRM), Metricool Pro (analytics), Make.com (automation), ManyChat (DM automation), Google Sheets (data layer), Looker Studio (dashboards), Substack (secondary distribution)

## Funnel
ManyChat mini-assessment (3-5 Qs in DMs) → Website full assessment (12 Qs built, targeting 20-25, with embedded WTP research) → BoldTrail CRM sequences (Hot 80-100 / Warm 50-79 / Future below 50)

## Content Pillars
1. Market Intelligence - local data, trends, predictions
2. Transaction Decoded - step-by-step buying/selling education
3. Lifestyle and Community - neighborhood guides, local business spotlights
4. Investment and Wealth Building - equity strategies, ROI, tax
5. Behind the Curtain - personal brand, day-in-life, transparent industry takes

## Geographic Focus
Pierce County (Tacoma, Bonney Lake, Graham, Puyallup, Eatonville), South King, Thurston, Kitsap counties. Burien (King County) for AFH.
