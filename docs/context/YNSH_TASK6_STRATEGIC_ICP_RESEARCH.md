### 1. Deep ICP Research (BLAC Framework)

For each ICP segment, classify their pain points using the **BLAC framework:**

- **B**latant — Pain they openly talk about, search for, complain about publicly
- **L**atent — Pain they feel but haven't articulated or don't consciously recognize yet
- **A**spirational — The solution represents something they WANT (lifestyle upgrade, dream outcome)
- **C**ritical — The solution addresses something they NEED (can't avoid, deadline-driven, crisis)

**Map each ICP on a 2x2 matrix: Blatant/Latent (x-axis) vs. Aspirational/Critical (y-axis)**

This tells us:
- Blatant + Critical = highest intent, easiest to reach (they're already searching)
- Latent + Critical = high value but requires education to activate
- Blatant + Aspirational = responsive to lifestyle marketing, social proof
- Latent + Aspirational = requires thought leadership to surface the desire

**ICP Segments to Research:**

1. **First-time buyers (25-38)** — Millennials/Gen Z in Pierce County
2. **Move-up buyers (35-55)** — Families outgrowing their starter home
3. **Sellers** — All ages, various motivations
4. **Seniors/downsizers (60+)** — Becca's differentiator segment
5. **Adult children managing parent transitions (38-55)** — The decision-maker for seniors
6. **Real estate investors** — Buy-and-hold, fix-and-flip, house hackers
7. **Military families** — JBLM proximity, PCS moves, VA loans
8. **Sphere/referral sources** — Past clients, friends, family, professional network

For EACH segment, document:
- Demographics (age, income, family status, location)
- Psychographics (values, fears, aspirations, identity)
- Trigger events (what moves them from passive to active)
- Information sources (where they research, who they trust)
- Decision timeline (how long from trigger to transaction)
- BLAC classification of their top 5 pain points
- Objections to working with an agent (and how to overcome)
- What "success" looks like to them (the dream outcome, Hormozi-style)

### 3. Willingness to Pay (WTP) Analysis

This informs both the assessment quiz (soft WTP questions) and Becca's pricing/positioning strategy.

**For the assessment quiz:** Design 2-3 questions that naturally surface budget/WTP without feeling invasive. In real estate, people often just state their price range, so we can be more direct than in other industries. But we want to understand not just "what can you afford" but "what are you willing to invest in the RIGHT agent experience."

**WTP Research Components:**

1. **Price sensitivity by segment** — What does each ICP consider "expensive" vs. "fair" vs. "good value" for a home in Pierce County? What's their actual purchasing power vs. stated budget?

2. **Service WTP** — Would clients pay for premium services (staging consultation, professional photography, dedicated project manager, senior transition coordination)? What's the perceived value gap between Becca's offering and a discount brokerage?

3. **Commission sensitivity** — How price-sensitive is each segment on commission? Who negotiates? Who doesn't care because they value the service?

4. **Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter** — Apply this framework to understand the "acceptable price range" for homes in each Pierce County submarket by ICP.

5. **Conjoint elements** — What service bundles would each ICP trade off? (e.g., Would a first-time buyer prefer a lower commission OR a concierge-level hand-holding experience?)

## ALEX HORMOZI FRAMEWORKS (Apply Throughout)

Becca owns all three Hormozi books. Incorporate these frameworks deeply into every deliverable:

**From $100M Offers:**
- **Value Equation:** Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement / Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice. For EVERY service Becca offers, maximize the numerator and minimize the denominator. Frame everything through this lens.
- **Grand Slam Offer construction:** What would make Becca's offer so good that people feel stupid saying no? Stack the value. What bonuses, guarantees, and risk-reversals can she add?
- **Naming and framing:** The assessment isn't a "form." It's a "5-Minute Readiness Score." The consultation isn't a "sales call." It's a "Custom Roadmap Session." Name everything to communicate value.
- **Scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees:** Where can these ethically apply to a relationship-based real estate business? (Not sleazy countdown timers. Real structural scarcity like limited bandwidth for new clients.)
- **Niche down to scale up:** Becca's niches (senior transitions, Pierce County, relationship-first) are her Grand Slam Offer differentiator. Don't try to be everything to everyone.

**From $100M Leads:**
- **The four core ways:** Warm outreach, cold outreach, content, paid ads. For a 25-deal/year relationship business, warm outreach and content should dominate. Cold and paid are supplemental.
- **Lead magnets as "value in advance":** The assessment quiz IS a lead magnet. The vendor directory IS value in advance. The neighborhood guides ARE value in advance. Each one should be so good that prospects feel indebted.
- **"More, better, new" framework:** Scale what works by doing MORE of it, doing it BETTER, then adding NEW channels. Don't add new channels until current ones are maxed.
- **The Lead-to-Client pipeline:** Engaged audience → lead magnet → nurture → offer → client. Map Becca's entire funnel to this.
- **Core four for warm outreach:** Private 1-on-1 messages, posting free content, warm reach-outs to past clients, referral requests. These are Becca's bread and butter.

**From $100M Money Models (apply to business model section):**
- **LTV > CAC:** Lifetime value of a real estate client (transaction + referrals generated + repeat business over 7-year cycle) vs. cost to acquire. Model this.
- **Pricing architecture:** How to position commission conversations. Value-framed, not cost-framed.
- **Ascension model:** Free content → assessment → consultation → client → repeat client → referral source. The three-business pipeline (education → home sale → care placement) IS an ascension ladder.
- **Retention and reactivation:** Past clients are the highest-value asset. What's the system to reactivate them every 12-18 months?

**Apply the Value Equation to each ICP segment:** For each ICP, explicitly calculate:
- What is their Dream Outcome? (Not "buy a house." The REAL dream outcome: "Feel confident I made the right decision for my family.")
- What increases Perceived Likelihood? (Social proof, credentials, Becca's track record, the assessment giving them clarity)
- What reduces Time Delay? (Becca's responsiveness, clear process, no wasted steps)
- What reduces Effort & Sacrifice? (Becca handles complexity, vendor recommendations, transition coordination)
