# 1. Project Overview

The Becca Pitts Marketing Engine is a self-reinforcing marketing ecosystem designed to position Becca as a micro-influencer and trusted real estate authority in Washington state. The system drives leads into her pipeline through multi-channel content, automated lead qualification, and data-driven content optimization.

## Mission Statement

Build a marketing machine where content drives assessment completions, assessment data informs content strategy, analytics validate performance, automation handles the data flow, and the CRM nurtures leads — all with minimal daily manual effort once built.

## Core Principles

- **First principles reasoning:** Every decision is derived from fundamental truths, not industry conventions.
- **Build the converter first, then the traffic:** The funnel must convert before we scale awareness.
- **80/20 content split:** 80% relatable/personal/community content, 20% professional/CTA content.
- **Own the audience:** Email list and CRM are primary. Third-party platforms are distribution, not home base.
- **Data over assumptions:** Every strategic decision is validated by performance data.
- **11 impressions in 90 days:** Every person in Becca's sphere sees her at least 11 times in 90 days across channels.

# 3. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

## Segment 1: First-Time Home Buyers

**Demographics:** Millennials and Gen Z, ages 25-38, single or newly partnered, income $50K-$120K

**Psychographics:** Anxious, overwhelmed by process, seeking trusted guidance, active on social media, influenced by authentic content over polished ads

**Primary channels:** Instagram (Reels, Stories), TikTok, YouTube Shorts

**Content that resonates:** Step-by-step education, myth-busting, "things I wish I knew," relatable anxiety content, success stories, checklists

**Buying triggers:** Rent increase, relationship milestone, peer homeownership, financial readiness, market conditions

## Segment 2: Seniors / Downsizers + Adult Children

**Demographics:** Boomers 60+, plus their adult children (Gen X/elder millennials, ages 38-55) who often initiate the research

**Psychographics:** Emotional decision (leaving family home), need patience and empathy, adult children doing online research, value trust and referrals over flashy marketing

**Primary channels:** Facebook (primary), YouTube, email newsletter, adult children also on Instagram/LinkedIn

**Content that resonates:** Downsizing guides, family planning worksheets, emotional transition content, local community resources, client testimonial stories, "helping mom and dad" content aimed at adult children

**Buying triggers:** Health changes, empty nest, maintenance burden, financial optimization, family proximity, lifestyle simplification

**Strategic insight:** This is Becca's primary differentiating ICP. Most agents ignore this segment or treat it transactionally. Becca's empathy-driven approach gives her a competitive advantage here.

## Segment 3: Move-Up / Second Home Buyers

**Demographics:** Gen X and elder millennials, ages 35-55, higher household income

**Psychographics:** More sophisticated, understand the process, want strategic guidance not hand-holding, think in terms of investment and lifestyle upgrade

**Primary channels:** LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram

**Content that resonates:** Market analysis, equity/wealth-building content, neighborhood comparisons, design/lifestyle upgrades, tax strategy

**Buying triggers:** Growing family, job change, equity milestone, lifestyle aspiration, investment opportunity

## Segment 4: Sellers / Listing Clients

**Demographics:** Cross-cuts all age groups

**Psychographics:** Want maximum value, concerned about timing, need confidence in pricing strategy and marketing plan

**Primary channels:** Facebook, LinkedIn, email newsletter, YouTube

**Content that resonates:** Market data, home value insights, staging tips, case studies of successful sales, pricing strategy education

**Buying triggers:** Market peak perception, life changes, financial need, relocation

# 5. Content Strategy

## Content Pillars

- **Pillar 1 — Market Intelligence:** Local market data, trends, predictions. Serves all segments. SEO-rich, shareable.
- **Pillar 2 — The Transaction Decoded:** Step-by-step education demystifying buying/selling. Primary: first-time buyers, sellers.
- **Pillar 3 — Lifestyle & Community:** Neighborhood guides, local business spotlights, community events. Builds local authority. Creates backlink opportunities.
- **Pillar 4 — Investment & Wealth Building:** Equity strategies, ROI analysis, tax implications. Primary: move-up buyers, investors.
- **Pillar 5 — Behind the Curtain:** Personal brand, day-in-the-life, client stories, transparent industry takes. Trust accelerator for all segments.

## Content Mix (80/20 Rule)

**50% Relatable/Personal:** Day-in-life, humor, pet content, local adventures, family moments, relatable situations. Purpose: earn follows, build connection, stay human.

**30% Community/Lifestyle:** Neighborhood spotlights, local business features, events, behind-the-scenes of WA life. Purpose: local authority, shareability, vendor backlinks.

**10-20% Professional/CTA:** Market data, listing showcases, client wins, assessment CTA, Hormozi-style CTA videos. Purpose: convert attention to pipeline.

## Hormozi-Style Video Format

- Length: 7-14 seconds maximum
- Structure: Hook (first 2 sec) → Content (5-8 sec) → Banner CTA overlay
- Banner CTA format: "Comment [KEYWORD] for [value]"
- Filming: Vertical, face-to-camera, bold text overlays, upbeat or trending music
- ManyChat integration: Comment keyword triggers automated DM sequence
- 10 video templates created in the content calendar spreadsheet

## The 1-to-10 Repurposing Framework

Every anchor piece (blog article or long-form video) spawns 10 derivative pieces: the original, an email feature, a carousel, a Reel/Short, a LinkedIn post, a quote graphic, a Story series, a TikTok repost, a podcast topic, and a Substack cross-post. Two anchor pieces per week = 20+ pieces of content.

# 6. Funnel Architecture

The funnel is two-tiered: ManyChat handles top-of-funnel qualification in DMs, the website assessment handles deep qualification with embedded willingness-to-pay research.

## Tier 1: ManyChat Mini-Assessment (3-5 Questions)

**Trigger:** User comments a keyword on Instagram/Facebook/TikTok post

**Delivery:** Automated DM sequence

**Duration:** Under 60 seconds to complete

**Purpose:** Warm the lead, deliver immediate value (PDF, guide, checklist), qualify intent, route to website assessment

### ManyChat Keywords

- **MARKET:** Spring 2026 WA market report + 2 qualifying questions
- **KEYS:** First-time buyer mini guide + encouragement
- **CHECKLIST:** Downloadable first-time buyer checklist
- **NEIGHBORHOOD:** Top 5 neighborhoods list with stats
- **INVEST:** Wealth-building through real estate guide
- **DREAM:** Homebuyer readiness guide
- **DOWNSIZE:** Senior downsizing guide + family planning worksheet
- **VALUE:** Free home value estimate offer

Every ManyChat sequence ends with a CTA to the full website assessment.

## Tier 2: Website Full Assessment (40 Questions, ~10 Minutes)

**Location:** Embedded in HTML landing page website (built by Claude)

**Format:** Interactive quiz-style with progress bar, step-by-step

**Purpose:** Deep lead qualification, willingness-to-pay data collection, voice-of-customer research, BoldTrail segmentation

### Assessment Architecture

**Act 1 — Engagement (Qs 1-3):** Easy, exciting questions that build investment. Goal/timeline/priorities.

**Act 2 — Intelligence Gathering (Qs 4-8):** Embedded WTP and VOC questions disguised as preference questions. Service model preference (anchors price tiers), agent selection criteria, past agent experience, biggest concerns.

**Act 3 — Qualification & Close (Qs 9-12):** Financial readiness, area interest, urgency, contact info with value proposition.

**Dedicated Senior/Downsizer Path:** Assessment branches when user selects downsizing/senior-related goals. Tailored questions about timeline, family involvement, next living situation, emotional readiness.

### Scoring & Routing

- **Hot (80-100):** Ready within 3 months, pre-approved/cash, wants to connect this week → Immediate personal outreach + priority BoldTrail sequence
- **Warm (50-79):** Interested but 3-6 months out, needs guidance → Nurture email sequence + weekly market updates via BoldTrail
- **Future (below 50):** Researching, 6+ months out → Long-term drip campaign with educational content via BoldTrail

Assessment webhook sends all response data to Google Sheets via Make.com for analytics.

# 8. Automation Pipeline (Make.com)

Optimized for Make.com free tier (1,000 ops/month, 2 active scenarios). Weekly cadence on Metricool pulls keeps operations low.

## Scenario 1: Weekly Metricool Pull (Scheduled, Sundays)

- Pulls performance data across all connected channels (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, GMB)
- Writes to Google Sheets (one tab per channel)
- ~10 operations/week = ~40 ops/month
- Looker Studio reads from these Sheets natively (no additional ops)

## Scenario 2: Unified Webhook Receiver (Always On)

- Single webhook URL receives data from both website assessment AND ManyChat
- Router module inspects incoming data and branches: assessment → Assessment tab, ManyChat → ManyChat tab
- ~3 ops per event (receive + route + write)
- At 50 assessments + 100 ManyChat events/month = ~450 ops/month

**Total estimated monthly operations:** ~490 out of 1,000 free tier limit

**Upgrade trigger:** When volume exceeds ~160 assessment + ManyChat events/month, upgrade to Core ($10.59/month, 10,000 ops, unlimited scenarios)

### Future Scenarios (Post-Upgrade)

- Scenario 3: BoldTrail sync — push assessment lead scores to CRM segments
- Scenario 4: Weekly content recommendation email — pulls top 5 performing posts, sends Becca a summary with recommendations
- Scenario 5: Metricool pixel website data → Sheets (once website and blog launch with pixel embedded)

# 13. Key Decisions Log

A record of all strategic decisions made during this project, with rationale.

- **Facebook is Tier 1 (not passive):** Becca's primary differentiating ICP (seniors/adult children) is most active on Facebook. Native content, not just Instagram cross-posts.
- **80/20 content split:** 80% relatable/community, 20% professional. People follow people, not brands. Professional content hits harder because it is rare.
- **Hormozi-style short-form:** 7-14 second videos with banner CTA overlay. Engineered for completion rate and ManyChat integration.
- **Two-tier assessment:** ManyChat mini (3-5 Qs) for low-friction top-of-funnel, website full (40 Qs) for deep qualification with WTP data.
- **One account per channel, not separate ICP accounts:** Avoids audience fragmentation, reduces management burden, builds momentum on single profiles.
- **BoldTrail email list is primary, Substack is secondary:** Own the list. Substack is for discovery only.
- **Make.com over n8n:** Easier to set up, free tier covers initial needs, clear upgrade path. Weekly Metricool pulls (not daily) to stay within free tier limits.
- **WTP questions disguised as preference questions:** "Which service model appeals to you most?" anchors different price tiers without feeling like a survey.
- **IDX as external link (noted limitation):** Property search traffic builds BoldTrail's domain authority, not Becca's. Investigate embedding IDX functionality to capture SEO benefit.
- **Tiered channel launch:** Tier 1 (Week 1) → Tier 2 (Week 5) → Tier 3 (Week 9). Validate each tier before expanding.
- **Chris Perry as closest content model:** 80/20 split, educational hooks, 53% sales lift. Study and adapt his approach.
