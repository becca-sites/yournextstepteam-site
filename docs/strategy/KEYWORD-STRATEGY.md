## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Strategy

Every FAQ uses FAQPage JSON-LD structured data, which feeds directly into Google's featured snippets and AI Overviews. The questions mirror real search queries, and the answers are concise enough to be pulled as direct answers.

## Copywriting Framework

The site uses a hybrid of three proven frameworks:

1. **Dan Kennedy's Direct Response**: Every section has a clear call-to-action. The hero uses urgency ("the longer you wait..."). Trust elements (15+ years, 500+ homes, 4.9 rating) are front and center.

2. **Alex Hormozi's Value Stack**: The assessment is positioned as a free, high-value offer. The "You'll Learn" list in the assessment promo section creates a value stack (market readiness score, timing guidance, neighborhood insights, personalized next steps).

3. **Donald Miller's StoryBrand**: Becca is the GUIDE, not the hero. The customer is the hero of the story. The copy follows the StoryBrand framework: Character (the homebuyer) has a Problem (overwhelmed, afraid of wrong decision), meets a Guide (Becca, with empathy + authority), who gives them a Plan (assessment, consultation), calls them to Action (take assessment, schedule call), and helps them avoid Failure (missed opportunities, overpaying) and achieve Success (dream home, confidence, peace of mind).
