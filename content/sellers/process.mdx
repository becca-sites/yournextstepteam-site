## 01. The valuation conversation

I come out to your home (or we hop on a video walk-through) and give you a real number. Not the highest possible price to win the listing, the realistic range based on what's actually closed in your neighborhood over the last 90 days.

By the end of the conversation you'll have a clear picture of what your home is worth, what changes (if any) would meaningfully move the number, and what timeline makes sense for what you're trying to accomplish.

## 02. The prep plan

Most homes need somewhere between zero and three weeks of prep. Sometimes it's a deep clean and decluttering. Sometimes it's paint, light fixtures, and minor repairs. Occasionally it's larger work.

We'll walk through your home together and I'll be honest about what's worth doing and what isn't. The goal is the highest sale price for the least invested time and cost, not the most beautiful version of your home.

## 03. Pricing strategy

The listing price is a strategic decision. We can price at market, just below to attract competition, or slightly above with room to negotiate. Each strategy works for specific market conditions and specific buyer pools. I'll tell you which one fits your situation and why.

## 04. Marketing

Once the home is ready, the marketing engine starts. Professional photos and floorplans. Listing on the MLS with full optimization. Distribution across {{ListingDistributionSites}}. Targeted social campaigns to the buyer pool most likely to transact. Open houses if and when they're strategically useful.

The marketing is calibrated to your specific home and the buyer who's actually going to write the offer.

## 05. Negotiating offers

When offers come in, we evaluate them together. Not just the price. The financing strength, the inspection contingency terms, the close date, the buyer's history. I'll tell you which offer is actually the strongest, even when it's not the highest price.

## 06. The path to closing

From accepted offer to closing day, expect three to five weeks. I'll handle the inspection negotiations, coordinate with the buyer's agent, manage the appraisal, and stay on top of the title work.

You should not have to chase anyone for updates. That's my job.
