The buying process with {{AgentFirstName}} runs in seven steps, from first conversation through closing. Most buyers go from initial call to closing in {{TypicalTimeline}} weeks, depending on financing and what comes on the market.

## 01. The first call

Twenty minutes, no pressure. We'll talk about where you are in the process, what you've been looking at, and what's on your shortlist. By the end of the call you'll have a clear sense of next steps and whether we're a fit to work together.

## 02. Financing setup

Before we tour, you need to know what you can carry. I'll connect you with two or three lenders I trust. Pick the one whose conversation feels best. Get pre-approved, not just pre-qualified.

## 03. The wishlist conversation

We sit down (in person, on a call, or over coffee) and translate your wishlist into actual search criteria. What's a must, what's a nice-to-have, what's a deal-breaker. This conversation saves you weeks of touring homes that were never going to work.

## 04. Touring

I set up showings, send you a curated list each week, and we tour together for the homes that pass the initial filter. I'll point out things on the walkthrough you'd miss on your own. Roof age, drainage issues, foundation movement, the things that turn into surprise costs.

## 05. Writing the offer

When the right home shows up, we move fast. I'll walk you through the comparable sales, the negotiating range, and the strategy. We'll write the offer and submit it the same day if needed.

## 06. Inspection, appraisal, and the closing details

This is where most surprises happen. I'll be on the ground with you through inspection negotiations, the appraisal, the title work, and the final walkthrough. Plan on three to five weeks from accepted offer to closing day.

## 07. Closing day

We sit at the closing table, you sign your name a few dozen times, and the keys are yours. I'll stick around afterward to help with the move-in details, the contractor referrals, and anything that comes up in the first 90 days.
