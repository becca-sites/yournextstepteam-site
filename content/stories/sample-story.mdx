## The situation

Two homes. Two buyers. Three sellers. A family that needed to sell their current home, buy the next one, and close on both inside a calendar week so the kids could start school in their new district on time.

The original timeline had four weeks of slack. By the time inspections finished, that slack was gone.

## What we did

The first call after inspection went to the listing agent on the home our clients were buying. The relationship from a closing six months prior carried weight, and the listing agent agreed to a contingency structure that gave both sides a clean fallback.

Pre-list prep on our clients' current home compressed from three weeks to seven days. The photographer rebooked. Staging came out of the same week. The listing went live on a Thursday.

Two competitive offers landed by Sunday. The one we recommended accepting was a hundred dollars below the highest, but it had a faster close and waived an inspection contingency the buyer had already done as a pre-offer walkthrough.

## The result

Both homes closed on the same Friday. Our clients moved in on the Saturday. School started Monday.

The lesson here is not the heroics. It is that a clear contract structure and a working relationship with the agent on the other side were the difference between a closed deal and a price reduction.
