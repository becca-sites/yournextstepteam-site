## A price map of the Eastside corridor

The corridor from Duvall west to Bellevue moves about $850,000 in median price across a 30-mile drive. That kind of spread inside one metro is unusual, and it is exactly why valley buyers and Eastside sellers keep crossing each other on the road.

Median price points by town this spring run roughly like this. Duvall and Carnation sit in the low $800s. Snoqualmie clears $900,000. Woodinville approaches $1.1M. Redmond and Sammamish push past $1.3M. Bellevue stretches into the $1.7M range and higher near the lake.

## Why the gap is bigger than it looks

The median price comparison hides three things that matter.

Lot size and acreage. A $850,000 home in Duvall often comes with a quarter acre or more, sometimes a full acre with horse setup. A $1.2M home in Redmond often comes with a townhouse footprint.

Commute. Bellevue and Redmond buyers regularly trade more square footage out in the valley for a longer drive. The math works for some families and not others.

Schools and amenities. Each district has its own profile. Lake Washington, Riverview, Snoqualmie Valley, and Bellevue all have different feeder patterns and different community feels.

## Who is actually moving where

Three patterns we keep seeing.

Eastside families trading townhouse footprint for valley acreage. The youngest kid hits school age and the calculus changes overnight.

Valley families trading lot size for commute time, usually when the second parent's job moves into the city.

Remote-first households planting in Carnation or Fall City because the trade-off no longer requires a daily drive into Bellevue.

## What it means for pricing strategy

If you are listing in the valley, your buyer pool includes Eastside escapees who are doing the same price-vs-acreage math you did when you bought. If you are listing on the Eastside, your buyer pool includes valley families who are willing to pay for the shorter commute. Knowing who is going to walk through the door changes how a home should be staged, photographed, and described.
