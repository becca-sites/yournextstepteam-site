## "Is it actually a good time to buy?"

It is a good time to buy if your numbers work and you plan to stay in the home for at least five years. Trying to time a market based on headlines is how buyers end up sitting on the sidelines for two years and missing the home they wanted. Rates may move. Prices may move. Your monthly payment is a function of your purchase price and your loan terms together, and a good lender can model both for you in an hour.

## "How much over asking should I offer?"

The right answer depends on the home and the competing offers. A blanket percentage is the wrong way to think about it. The strongest offers we have written this spring used escalation clauses with carefully chosen caps, clean financing terms, and contingency timelines that made sense for the seller. Sometimes that means a small bump over asking. Sometimes that means a substantial one. The structure matters more than the ceiling.

## "Do I really need a buyer agent if I can find listings on my phone?"

Finding a home is the easy part. The harder parts come after you fall in love with one. Inspection negotiations, appraisal gaps, contract language, financing contingencies, repair requests, title surprises, and the quiet moments in escrow where deals fall apart. A buyer agent is your representation in all of that. The listing agent is the seller's, and they cannot serve both sides equally without trade-offs.

## "What is the school situation in the valley?"

Riverview School District covers Duvall, Carnation, and Fall City. It runs small class sizes and ranks well on outcomes. Snoqualmie Valley School District covers Snoqualmie and North Bend. Monroe School District covers Monroe. Each district has its own personality and feeder patterns, and we are happy to walk through specifics for the streets you are looking at.

## "How long does the whole process take?"

From the day you pick a home and write an offer, a typical closing in this market runs 30 to 45 days. Pre-approval up front can shave a week. Inspection, appraisal, and lender conditions are the three big timelines inside the contract. We map the whole thing out on day one so there are no surprises.
