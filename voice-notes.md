- **Celebrates milestones genuinely:** "We are no longer 'pending inspection' we are straight up 'pending' 🎊"
- **Honest about what she doesn't know:** "Not sure how or if that could work with multiple purchasers or if the VA even does land"

- **High standards for the profession:** "Some agents these days.... Good lord, this career is not for you."

### Communication Patterns
- **Updates proactively** — doesn't wait to be asked: "The appraisal has been ordered" / "They signed. We are mutual."
- **Sets clear expectations:** "Closing COULD happen as early as 9 or 10......or it could be as late at 5pm."
- **Adds context to good news:** "I have a small amount of news which I count as good."
- **Explains the WHY:** "The due date is not the same as the access date. I don't know when they're coming to do their walk-through yet"
- **Refers to specialists openly:** "Contact Carina for more detailed loan questions... She'll be able to answer your questions a lot more efficiently than me"
- **Follows up without nagging:** Checks in with lenders, agents, clients at natural intervals
